Welcome to the Artificial Immune System (AIS) learning portal.

Inspired by the human immune system, AIS offers innovative solutions for anomaly detection and optimization problems in computing. This portal is designed for Computer Science (CS) and IT students—those familiar with algorithm design and computational concepts, but who may not have deep biology knowledge. Here, you’ll discover how biological principles can enhance computational models and expand your understanding of bio-inspired algorithms.
