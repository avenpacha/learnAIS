About the Course

WELCOME TO THIS INTERACTIVE LEARNING JOURNEY ABOUT ARTIFICIAL IMMUNE SYSTEMS (AIS)!

This course is designed to guide you step by step through the fascinating world of AIS, explaining how it draws inspiration from the human immune system and how it’s applied in computing and data analysis.

COURSE STRUCTURE:

  • Lesson 1: What is AIS?
  • Lesson 2: How CSA Works
  • Lesson 3: Applications
  • Lesson 4: Interactive Demo

Each lesson includes clear explanations, engaging visuals, and practical examples to help you understand the key concepts.

HOW TO NAVIGATE:

  • Click the Forward button at the bottom of each page to move to the next lesson.
  • You can also use the navigation bar at the top or the Quick Links in the footer to jump directly to any lesson.

Tip: Take your time exploring each lesson and make sure to try out the Interactive Demo at the end to see AIS in action!
